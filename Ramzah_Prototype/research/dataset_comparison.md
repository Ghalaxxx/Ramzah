## Decision

Use a small verified KArSL healthcare vocabulary, initialize from downloaded KArSL SignBart weights, and adapt using **newly extracted landmarks with the exact deployed extractor**. This avoids pretending Kinect joints, arbitrary Kaggle pose arrays and MediaPipe joints are interchangeable. Keep RGB as the next ablation: it preserves facial expression and appearance cues lost by the 48 selected pose points.

KSU-SSL is the preferred future Saudi healthcare adaptation corpus if licensed access becomes available. Do not pool classes across languages merely because Arabic gloss strings match. A Saudi Deaf reviewer must verify sign equivalence and label meaning. Do not pretrain isolated labels using unsegmented continuous Isharah clips.

The local prototype uses a selected subset of official **test** archives, repurposed into development splits, to make a compact reproducible experiment. This is **not** a reproduction of the original benchmark. The upstream ALL checkpoint may have seen these clips and signers. Any subsequent reported score is an adaptation diagnostic, not evidence of unseen-signer generalization. A new participant cohort remains necessary.

Exact native-video counts, corruption, duplicates, duration/FPS/resolution distributions and examples are generated by `scripts/inspect_dataset.py` under `reports/dataset/`. Do not extrapolate the local audit to the complete 75,300-clip corpus.
